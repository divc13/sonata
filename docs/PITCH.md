## The premise

> Every song has an original recording, the master. In Sonata, the master is a
> one-of-one NFT — a digital deed — that controls a real NEAR account.

| Piece         | Plain meaning                                          |
| ------------- | ------------------------------------------------------ |
| Master        | the original recording, the file every copy comes from |
| NFT           | the digital deed that proves who owns it               |
| Song account  | the song's digital bank account                        |
| No login key  | nobody can sign in and move the money                  |
| Verified hour | only time actually spent playing                       |

## Final narration

**[0:00–0:15]**

This is Sonata. It is a music app. You pay five dollars a month, and you can
play every song in the catalogue. Let me show you how it works.

**[0:15–0:42]**

Here is a song. Without the pass, I get a thirty-second preview. I buy the
pass — five dollars for thirty days — and the full recording unlocks. As I
listen, Sonata verifies the time. The player only counts time actually played.
The song earns three cents for every hour. Every song has an original
recording: the master. The master is the file every copy comes from.

**[0:42–1:08]**

In Sonata, the master is a one-of-one NFT — a digital deed. The deed controls
a small NEAR account: a digital bank account for the song. It has no login key.
Only the current owner can withdraw. The account's balance and future income
follow the deed. The chain tab shows the song account and the current owner.

**[1:08–1:30]**

This is the artist's dashboard. Each song shows what it earned and who owns it.
The balance is read live from the song account. Nadia withdraws her balance.
This withdraw is a real testnet transaction. The timeline records every owner,
every sale, and every payout. The history is a receipt for every event.

**[1:30–2:04]**

Now the buyer's side. The marketplace lists masters for sale. I buy this one.
The buy is a real testnet transaction. The NFT moves to me, and the song
account moves with it — the balance and all future income. The old owner tries
to withdraw and is rejected. I can manage the master now. Switch the viewer to
Nadia: the old owner is blocked. Switch back to the collector; the holder view
returns. Relist the master at fifty dollars so five listings remain.

**[2:04–2:21]**

Five dollars a month. Three cents per verified hour. A four-hour daily cap. At
the cap, the artist earns three dollars sixty; Sonata keeps one dollar forty
before costs. Five percent of every resale goes to the creator.

**[2:21–2:42]**

Twelve masters are live on NEAR testnet. These are real transactions. Past
earnings are historical data. The catalogue, the accounts and the marketplace
are live on testnet. Sonata. Every song is an account.
